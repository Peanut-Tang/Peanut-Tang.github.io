About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the Center on Frontiers of Computing Studies (CFCS), Peking University (PKU), under the supervision of Prof. Kuan Cheng. Prior to this, I received my B.E. degree in Computer Science and Technology from the School of the Gifted Young, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Xue Chen.

Overall, I have a broad interest in theoretical computer science (TCS), particularly in areas related to pseudorandomness, computational complexity and cryptography. I have conducted research on the design of PRGs, hash functions and error-correcting codes. At the same time, I am passionate about exploring combinatorics and probability in pure mathematics, as well as optimization problems in computational or financial mathematics.

In Summer and Fall 2025, I visited Prof. Xin Li's group at Johns Hopkins University (JHU) as a research intern. Currently, I continue to work with Prof. Xin Li remotely.
